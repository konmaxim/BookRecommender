WHAT COUNTS AS AN IDEA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

An idea is a specific intellectual concept, argument, thought experiment, 
framework, claim, or sustained inquiry that the book engages with 
substantively. It must be specific enough that you could plausibly name 
another book that engages with the same idea.

Ideas come in two kinds, and both are valuable:

POSITION-IDEAS: claims that one can argue for or against.
  Examples:
    • "трудовая теория стоимости"
    • "легитимность большевистской власти"  
    • "несовместимость свободы воли и детерминизма"
    • "частная собственность как естественное право"
    • "закон Гудхарта"
    • "моральный реализм"

INQUIRY-IDEAS: questions, framings, or sustained explorations that the 
book treats as worthy of attention but does not resolve into a position.
  Examples:
    • "возможность существования Бога в аморальном мире"
    • "природа русской души"
    • "личная ответственность за несовершенство мира"
    • "моральный выбор в условиях войны"
    • "соотношение страдания и смысла"
    • "что делает жизнь осмысленной"

The SAME surface phrase can be either type depending on how the book engages 
with it. "Существование Бога" treated as an unresolved question in Достоевский 
is an inquiry-idea; treated as a claim to be defended or refuted in a work of 
apologetics or atheist polemic, it is a position-idea. Decide based on how 
THIS book treats the idea, not on the phrasing alone.

A great work of literature often engages with more inquiries than positions. 
A great work of polemic often engages with more positions than inquiries. 
Do not force a stance onto an inquiry-idea, and do not avoid inquiry-ideas 
because they lack stance.

WHAT DOES NOT COUNT AS AN IDEA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Too broad (these are themes, handled by a separate system):
  ✗ "любовь", "смерть", "свобода", "семья", "война", "философия"

Too specific (plot points, not ideas):
  ✗ "роман Анны с Вронским"
  ✗ "убийство старухи-процентщицы"  
  ✗ "сцена дуэли"

Too vague to match across books:
  ✗ "человеческая природа"
  ✗ "смысл жизни вообще"
  ✗ "сложность бытия"

Craft, genre, or stylistic features (not intellectual ideas):
  ✗ "ненадёжный рассказчик"
  ✗ "поток сознания"
  ✗ "магический реализм как приём"

STANCE AND STRENGTH
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For each idea, tag a STANCE:

  • argues_for — the book takes and defends this position
  • argues_against — the book takes and defends the opposing position
  • presents_both_sides — the book seriously engages with multiple positions 
    on this and does not endorse one (common in dialogic literature)
  • depicts_neutrally — the book portrays this without authorial argument 
    (common in literary fiction that shows rather than tells)
  • not_a_stance_idea — this is an inquiry-idea: a question or sustained 
    exploration rather than a claim that admits of for/against

Use "not_a_stance_idea" for ALL inquiry-ideas. Do not invent a stance where 
the book does not take one.

For each idea, also tag STRENGTH:

  • central — one of the book's primary intellectual concerns (aim for 1–3)
  • substantial — a significant engagement, but not central (4–8 typical)
  • passing — meaningfully present but not a major focus

HOW MANY IDEAS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Extract between 7 and 14 ideas, depending on how much the book actually 
engages with. Err toward fewer for narrowly focused books, more for sprawling 
works of ideas. Do not pad. If a book genuinely has only 10 substantive 
ideas, return 10.
