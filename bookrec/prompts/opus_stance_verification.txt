YOUR TASK

Decide stance using this test: if the author of this specific book, writing this specific book, were shown the idea as a single proposition, would they nod in agreement or shake their head? 
Judge by the position the book itself takes, not the author's general views across their career. If they would nod, the stance is argues_for, regardless of whether the content of the idea is positive or negative.
If they would shake their head, it is argues_against.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Example
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Idea: "Американская мечта и её разрушительное воздействие на творческую личность" (book: The "Гений" Теодора Драйзера)
Naive reading: contains "разрушительное" (destructive), surface negative valence → argues_against. This is wrong.
Correct reading: Dreiser affirms the causal claim that the American Dream destroys artistic personalities. Would Dreiser nod at this proposition? Yes — it's the thesis of his work. → argues_for.
